# Trophy Tracker challenge library

The challenge library is an independent personal tracker. Official certification remains with each governing organization.

Programs currently represented:

- Ultimate Waterfowlers Challenge — 41 North American waterfowl targets
- Utah DWR Waterfowl Ultimate Slam — 19 targets
- NWTF Turkey Slams — Grand, Royal and World milestones
- Utah DWR Upland Game Ultimate Slam — 15 species
- Nebraska Upland Slam — 4 species
- Washington Turkey Slam — Eastern, Merriam's and Rio Grande
- GSCO Super Ten — 10 North American big-game categories
- GSCO Super Slam — current 29-target application structure
- GSCO Grand Slam of North American Wild Sheep
- GSCO Ovis and Capra World Slams — library placeholders pending dedicated international species packs

The app intentionally separates personal `harvested` status from organization-specific documentation/certification and state-program qualification.

A single canonical trophy record is reused anywhere the same species qualifies. State-specific slams use a qualification flag so, for example, a Ring-necked Pheasant can be one trophy record while only counting toward Utah or Nebraska when the user marks that harvest as eligible for that program.
